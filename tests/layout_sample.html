1. Summary

The company grew revenue by twelve percent this year, driven by strong demand in the western region and a new partnership with Acme Trading. Costs stayed flat while margins improved across every product line, and the new warehouse opened two months ahead of schedule.

Customer satisfaction rose to 94 percent, the highest score since the survey began. Staff turnover fell for the third year in a row, and training hours per employee doubled. The board approved a dividend of ₹ 12 per share.
