How it works

1. Home survey

One of our assessors visits your home for about two hours. We measure every room, check the walls, loft and windows, and look at your recent energy bills. You receive a written report within five working days, with costs and expected savings for each option.

2. Design and quote

If you decide to go ahead, our engineers produce a detailed design and a fixed quote. There are no hidden extras: the quote includes scaffolding, electrical work, building notifications and removal of old equipment.

3. Installation

Most heat pump installations take three to five days and solar installations one to two. We protect floors and furniture, keep you informed each day and leave the site clean. You will have heating and hot water every night during the work.

4. Aftercare

We register your system for the relevant guarantees and grants, show you how to use the controls and call you after the first month to check everything is working. Annual servicing is available from £120.
